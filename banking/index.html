FAQ

How long does a bank penetration test take?

A focused test takes 1–2 weeks; a full assessment of core, channels and network takes 4–6 weeks depending on scope.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before any work begins, and all findings stay strictly confidential.

Will the test disrupt banking operations?

No. Testing is non-destructive and controlled. Critical findings are reported to you immediately.

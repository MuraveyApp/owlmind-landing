Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what clients ask before starting.

How does the audit work?

We start with an NDA and a scoping call. Then we test your systems from multiple angles — attacker, auditor, and architect. Every finding is manually verified, and critical issues are reported to you immediately.

How long does it take?

An express check takes about a week, a standard audit 2–3 weeks, and a full enterprise assessment 4–6 weeks — depending on the number of systems.

What do I get at the end?

A report in three formats (executive, technical, compliance) with every vulnerability rated by severity, a video proof-of-concept for critical and high findings, and a code-level fix for each one.

Is my data safe? Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before any work begins, and all findings and data stay strictly confidential. Client names are never disclosed without permission.

Will the testing break my systems?

No. Testing is controlled and non-destructive. We do not modify data, delete anything, or disrupt service. Critical findings are reported to you first, not exploited for impact.

Do you also fix the vulnerabilities?

We provide code-level remediation for every finding and 30 days of post-engagement support with a free re-scan of fixed items. We can also work alongside your developers.

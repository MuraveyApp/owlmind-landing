FAQ

How long does a casino security audit take?

A focused audit takes 1–2 weeks; a full platform audit (games + payments + panels) takes 2–4 weeks depending on scope.

Do you test live games or just the platform?

Both. We test the game logic and RNG, plus the surrounding platform — payments, real-time channels, and admin panels.

Will the audit disrupt my players?

No. Testing is non-destructive and does not affect live play. Critical findings are reported to you first.
